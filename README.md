## Umay Şamlı

**AI safety & security researcher** · evaluations · agentic misalignment · adversarial AI

I build evaluations of agentic misalignment and test whether models recognise them. Before research, I worked in security engineering at NATO SHAPE and NATO HQ.

**Now:** Researcher at TED University (M.Sc. Computer Engineering) · visiting researcher at the TEDU Autonomous Systems Lab, in Dr. Emin Kuğu's lab · in my free time, software engineer at [Hanami Products](https://github.com/hanamiproducts)

### Research

- [**Evaluation awareness in a published insider-threat test**](https://github.com/samliumay/do_old_insider_LLM_tests_still_work_today): re-runs Anthropic's Agentic Misalignment scenarios on open-weight models; recent models often say they are being tested. Paper in preparation · [write-up](https://umaysamli.com/blog/are-old-insider-llm-tests-reliable)
- [**Self-logs and double-entry logging**](https://github.com/samliumay/how_reliable_models_self_logs): do agents' own action logs match what they did? Pilot done, follow-ups at benchmark stage · [pilot write-up](https://umaysamli.com/blog/trusting-models-to-log-their-own-actions)
